Proposed ViT-Guided CNN Architecture

Rather than naively concatenating dense embeddings, the ViT-Guided CNN uses Vision Transformer attention rollout to spatially guide convolutional feature extraction, combined with FiLM channel modulation and cross-attention fusion.

1. Preprocessing Stage (Native 224×224 Crop)

Native-resolution center/random crop from 256×256 without resampling to preserve high-frequency artifacts. Feed identical tensors to both branches.

↓ Dual-Branch Feature Extraction ↓

CNN Branch: EfficientNet-B0

Role: Captures localized pixel-level texture irregularities, noise residuals, and upsampling artifacts.

Modification: Features modulated by ViT spatial gating F' = F * (1 + σ(conv(A))) and FiLM affine parameters (γ, β).

ViT Branch: DINOv2 ViT-S/14

Role: Global self-attention across 16×16 non-overlapping patches (256 tokens) with dynamic positional embedding.

Guidance: Attention rollout generates spatial importance map A, and CLS token generates FiLM channel modulation vectors.

↓ Cross-Attention & Stream Fusion ↓

Cross-Attention Fusion & Frequency Stream

CNN feature tokens cross-attend to ViT patch tokens. High-pass SRM (Spatial Rich Model) filters extract high-frequency noise residuals.

Output = [pool(CNN) || CLS_token || SRM_residual] → MLP Head → Calibrated Logit (BCE with Logits)

Executive Summary

DeepTruth AI implements genuine machine learning models trained on 140,000 real and fake face images (FFHQ vs StyleGAN). By combining spatial attention rollout from DINOv2 with local feature extraction from EfficientNet-B0 and an SRM high-pass frequency filter, the proposed ViT-Guided CNN achieves an AUC of 0.9560 on the 20,000-image test set, outperforming both standalone backbones.
